Questions

What counts as commercial use?

Any use in a product, service, or tool that generates revenue, directly or indirectly. This includes SaaS applications, paid products, client projects, and internal business tools. If your organisation makes money and uses Tempis, you need a commercial licence.

Can I try it before buying?

Yes. The library is fully functional without a licence. Use it freely for evaluation, prototyping, and non-commercial work. When you're ready to ship commercially, purchase a licence.

Is the licence per developer or per project?

Per organisation. A single licence covers your entire team, with unlimited developers, unlimited projects, and no seat limits.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Cancel whenever you like. Your licence remains valid until the end of the current billing period. No lock-in, no penalties.

Can I use Tempis in a SaaS product?

Yes, with a commercial licence. The licence covers embedding Tempis in any product you distribute or host, including SaaS applications served to your customers.

Do I get access to the source code?

Yes. Tempis is source-available. Both community and commercial users have full access to the source code.

What support is included?

Commercial licence holders get priority email support for bug reports, integration questions, and feature requests. Community users can use GitHub issues.

Can I pay annually?

Not yet, but annual billing is coming soon. Get in touch if you'd prefer to pay upfront for the year.

Something not covered here?

Email us about enterprise terms, invoicing, or anything the FAQ misses. See also the terms and privacy policy.
