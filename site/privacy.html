Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Summary

We collect minimal data, we don't track you across the web, and we never sell your information.

What We Collect

Website visitors

This website does not use cookies, analytics scripts, or tracking pixels. We do not collect personal data from visitors browsing the documentation or examples.

Our hosting provider (Cloudflare) may collect basic server logs (IP address, page requested, timestamp) as part of standard web infrastructure. This data is not accessible to us and is subject to Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Licence purchasers

When you purchase a commercial licence, our payment provider (Lemon Squeezy) collects:

  • Name and email address
  • Billing information (processed securely by Lemon Squeezy — we do not store card details)
  • Organisation name (if provided)

This information is used solely to process your payment, deliver your licence, and provide support. It is subject to Lemon Squeezy's privacy policy.

Email contact

If you email us at info@tempis.dev, we retain your email address and message content for the purpose of responding to your enquiry and providing support.

What We Don't Do

  • We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties
  • We do not use analytics or tracking tools on this website
  • We do not send marketing emails unless you explicitly opt in
  • The Software itself does not collect, transmit, or phone home any data

Data Retention

Purchase records are retained for as long as your licence is active and for a reasonable period afterward for accounting purposes. Email correspondence is retained indefinitely for support continuity unless you request deletion.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal data we hold about you by emailing info@tempis.dev.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email info@tempis.dev.
